Designed as a learning loop

Explore. Experiment. Reflect.

VCLabS turns a chemistry lesson into a guided 3D experience. Students build confidence through exploration, then use feedback to understand what happened.

  1. 01

    Get oriented

    The Tutorial Room introduces controls, safety and lab etiquette.

  2. 02

    Discover

    The Apparatus Room turns unfamiliar equipment into recognisable tools.

  3. 03

    Apply

    The Experiment Room guides students through a practical procedure.

  4. 04

    Check understanding

    The Quiz Room turns mistakes into useful feedback.

The project

A virtual space for safer, clearer chemistry learning.

Built by a student team, VCLabS combines 3D exploration with guided learning for secondary school students. It gives learners a place to practise the sequence of a simple experiment before applying that understanding in the real lab.
